## Niche and conversion fit

The site sells Philippines-based staffing and outsourcing support. Its established Blog pattern helps an operating owner define a bounded, evidence-led work queue before discussing staffing through `/services` or `/contact-us`. The October 5 topics therefore concentrate on recurring administrative, customer-support, ecommerce, finance-operations, workforce-operations, and content-operations decisions that a buyer can safely delegate while retaining policy, money, legal, security, and release authority.

The proposed slugs were compared with the tracked Blog and Research corpus at baseline. None is already present. Topic selection also avoids the September 28 and October 2 families, including payroll change evidence, access-request fulfillment, inventory adjustments, commission statements, report distribution, carrier damage claims, cash forecasts, vendor scorecards, support taxonomies, asset expiration, interview scheduling, review-comment registers, product bundles, test-data resets, and goodwill credits.

## Originality design

Each article will use a topic-specific structure and worked example rather than a common sequence with swapped nouns:

1. Customer corrections: request-to-field lineage and identity ambiguity.
2. Insurance tracking: policy-period continuity, endorsement gaps, and supplier-owner review.
3. Knowledge-base retirement: claim inventory, dependency mapping, redirects, and search verification.
4. Cancellation packets: channel chronology, effective date, recurring charge state, and owner remedy.
5. Returns inspection: custody, condition vocabulary, image evidence, and disposition separation.
6. Deliverable acceptance: obligation-to-artifact mapping, version freezes, comments, and sign-off state.
7. Equipment returns: asset identity, shipping custody, data handling, accessories, and closure.
8. Order holds: reason-code isolation, safe evidence collection, owner release, and downstream checks.
9. Invoice completeness: document matrix, purchase-order lineage, receipt evidence, duplicates, and exceptions.
10. Suppression synchronization: event precedence, channel scope, propagation lag, and proof-of-exclusion.
11. Listing discrepancies: field-level source authority, channel snapshots, variant inheritance, and correction verification.
12. Outage updates: verified-fact clock, audience segmentation, message approval, cadence, and closure.

Before integration, the audit will calculate five-word-shingle overlap within the Blog family and against the prior corpus, and will separately inspect repeated substantive sentences, paragraphs, section sequences, and worked examples. Numeric overlap is supporting evidence only; qualitative repetition requires rewriting.
